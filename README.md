# ERealtor AI Journey

## Our Mission

To modernize our real estate business with the help of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, save time, generate better leads, and make daily operations more efficient.

## 90-Day Goal

To build an AI-powered system that can work as a digital assistant for the ERealtor real estate business.

## Tasks We Want AI to Help With

- Find new property listings
- Collect property listing details
- Create property marketing posts
- Create content for Facebook and Instagram
- Generate video scripts
- Assist with initial WhatsApp replies
- Maintain lead records
- Provide follow-up reminders
- Generate daily reports
- Automate content publishing between Instagram and Facebook

## Our Principle

Every day, we aim to build at least one thing that can be used in the real business or can save us time.

## Project Duration

90 Days
